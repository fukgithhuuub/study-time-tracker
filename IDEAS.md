# Future Functionality Ideas for StudyFlow

To make the app more powerful and engaging, here are several ideas for future development:

## 1. Focus Mode & Distraction Blocking
- **Focus Soundscapes:** Integrated white noise, lo-fi beats, or ambient nature sounds.
- **Strict Mode:** Prevent switching tabs or minimize the browser during a Pomodoro session (browser extension needed).

## 2. Gamification & Motivation
- **Study Levels:** Earn XP for every hour studied and "level up" your profile.
- **Badges:** Unlock achievements for milestones (e.g., "10 Hour Week", "Early Bird", "Pomodoro Master").
- **Avatars:** A digital pet or character that grows as you study more.
- **Leaderboards:** Friendly competition with friends or global users.

## 3. Advanced Planning
- **Exam Countdown:** Track dates for upcoming exams with a "Days Remaining" widget.
- **Subject-Specific Goals:** Set monthly or weekly hour targets per subject.
- **Smart Scheduling:** Suggest study times based on when you are most productive.

## 4. Collaborative Features
- **Study Rooms:** Join a virtual room with others to study together (coworking style).
- **Resource Sharing:** Upload and share notes or flashcards within subjects.
- **Study Partners:** Find people studying the same subjects.

## 5. Better Analytics
- **Heatmap:** A GitHub-style contribution graph for study consistency.
- **Productivity Score:** A score calculated based on session length and consistency.
- **Correlation Insights:** Show how break length affects subsequent study session focus.

## 6. Integrations
- **Google Calendar/Outlook:** Sync study sessions as calendar events.
- **Notion/Obsidian:** Export study logs or link to relevant notes.
- **Spotify:** Control music playback directly from the timer tab.

## 7. Wellness & Health
- **Stretch Reminders:** Prompts to do simple stretches during breaks.
- **Hydration Tracker:** Reminders to drink water during long study blocks.
- **Eye Strain Alert:** 20-20-20 rule reminders (every 20 mins, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds).
